I am a multidisciplinary technology professional with 12+ years of experience 
across business analysis, technical writing, software development, and delivery 
management. What sets me apart is not just the breadth of my experience but how 
each discipline has deepened the others.

As a Senior Business Analyst, I bridge the gap between business stakeholders and 
technical teams, ensuring requirements are clear, complete, and actionable. As a 
Technical Writer, I have spent 8+ years making complex systems accessible, a skill 
that sharpens every document, specification, and stakeholder communication I produce. 
My software development background means I engage with engineering teams with 
credibility and precision. And my delivery management experience means I understand 
not just what needs to be built, but how to get it across the finish line.

Most recently I spent four years at Google, working embedded within data and AI 
product teams across Google Cloud, an experience that sharpened my ability to operate 
at scale, maintain high quality standards, and collaborate across global, 
cross-functional teams.

I thrive in environments that value clear thinking, strong communication, and 
cross-functional collaboration, whether that is helping a business define what to 
build, helping users understand how to use it, or helping customers get maximum 
value from a product.

Currently based in Wellington and open to opportunities across business analysis, 
technical writing, customer success, and project delivery anywhere in New Zealand 
or remotely.

Open to a conversation, feel free to connect.
